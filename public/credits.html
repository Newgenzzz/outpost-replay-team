NASA data and scientific references

The game separates observations, published research, engineering references and training assumptions. Open Science (Elm in Azerbaijani) or Rewind & Compare → Mission science and assumptions → Citations for the full in-game reference list.

  • NASA DONKI notification 20240516-7D-001: archived May 2024 space-weather context for the shielding lesson. Optional recent flare records are a separate catalog; they do not alter mission outcomes or supply crew-dose measurements.
  • NASA OSDR VEG-01A lettuce trial: the ISS water-stress finding motivates crop-water planning. The game's crop duration, costs and yield are assumptions.
  • NASA LRO/LOLA-derived illumination research, Gläser et al.: two published site-planning rows support the sunlight/storage comparison. The 3D crater is procedural teaching art, not a NASA elevation dataset; its visibility index does not drive mission power.
  • NASA life-support baseline document (BVAD): reference crew oxygen consumption. ISS water-recovery research is shown separately from the game's simplified recycler.
  • LCROSS Cabeus ice estimate: a reference fraction for fictional extraction feedstock, not an ice measurement at this outpost.
  • NOAA GOES May 2024 proton peak: the default scenario reuses the 207 pfu near-Earth peak only. Its lunar timing, shielding and flux-to-dose conversion are assumptions. NOAA is a separate agency; this is not counted as an additional NASA dataset.

No RadLab dosimetry series, Mars Trek terrain, or raw LOLA/Diviner raster is bundled. Mars mission values are teaching scenarios. The model is unsuitable for operational mission planning or astronaut exposure forecasts.
